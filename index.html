Greek Yogurt

Day 1

Things to buy:

  • Milky Mist or Epigamia unflavored Greek yogurt.
  • Whole milk (nattile or veettile pashuvinte milk) or Amul Gold. The milk should be fatty and rich.

Prep:

Boil the milk to 86°C and let the temperature drop to 46°C. Take 2 tablespoons of Greek yogurt and mix it with a small amount of the warm milk. Later, mix this well with the rest of the milk and put it in a tight, hygienic container. Let it sit for 12-14 hours.

Day 2

When you look at the mixture, you might see a curd-like but slimy texture. Don't worry—let's prep for the next step.

Prep:

  • Cheesecloth or a good cotton cloth to drain.

Take a big vessel; we need to hang the mixture and leave room for the water to drain.

Put it in the fridge overnight. When you put it in the fridge, the fermentation will stop.

Take it out from the fridge and mix well (the sliminess will go away with the drained water, just mix the rest well).

Ta-da, ready!
